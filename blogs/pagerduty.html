Connecting PagerDuty Incident Intelligence & On-Call Topology to Cognee

By Bhomvrat Rai • Issue #6288 • 5 min read

PagerDuty serves as the mission-critical incident dispatch and on-call escalation nervous system for modern technology enterprises. However, post-incident reviews and site reliability analytics often suffer from fragmented context scattered across siloed tickets and logs. By integrating PagerDuty incidents, service catalogs, and on-call responder assignments directly into Cognee's AI Memory Engine, engineering teams unlock Graph RAG reasoning for proactive reliability engineering.

Key Architectural Capabilities

  • Incident & Alert Graph Mapping: Synchronizes incident titles, urgency levels (high, low), states (triggered, acknowledged, resolved), and timestamps into interconnected document entities.
  • Service Topology Context: Automatically correlates outages with underlying microservices and operational catalog entries.
  • On-Call Responder Attribution: Associates incident resolution timelines with assigned responders and escalation policies for intelligent post-mortem synthesis.
  • Deterministic Entity Hashing: Every document features a deterministic SHA-256 ID ensuring zero duplicate insertions across continuous synchronization cycles.

Usage Example

from cognee_community_connector_pagerduty import PagerDutyConnector

# Initialize connector with environment token
connector = PagerDutyConnector(
    api_token="your_pagerduty_api_token",
    statuses=["triggered", "acknowledged"],
    urgencies=["high"],
    include_services=True
)

# Ingest high-urgency incidents into Cognee knowledge graph
documents = connector.fetch_documents(limit=50)

for doc in documents:
    print(f"Ingested {doc['metadata']['type']}: {doc['metadata'].get('service_name')} (ID: {doc['id'][:8]}...)")
