Paystack Payment Transactions & Refunds Connector for Cognee

By Bhomvrat Rai • October 30, 2026 • topoteretes/cognee#6390

Architectural Overview

African merchants and modern tech companies across Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, and Kenya use Paystack to process billions in transactions. However, autonomous agents in customer support, operations, and finance currently lack visibility into merchant payment histories. This connector indexes transactions, customer details, and refunds into Cognee Knowledge Graphs.

Key Capabilities

  • Transaction Intelligence: Maps references, amounts, currencies (NGN, GHS, ZAR, KES, USD), customer emails, and channels.
  • Refund Correlation: Associates refund events with initial transaction nodes to maintain complete ledger transparency.
  • Subunit Normalization: Automatically converts kobo/cents to standard major financial denominations.

Usage Example

from cognee_community_connector_paystack import PaystackConnector

connector = PaystackConnector(secret_key="sk_live_...")
documents = connector.fetch_documents(page_size=50)

print(f"Ingested {len(documents)} financial documents into Cognee.")
for doc in documents[:2]:
    print(doc["metadata"])
    print(doc["text"])
