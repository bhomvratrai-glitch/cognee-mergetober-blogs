Airwallex Global Payouts & Treasury Connector for Cognee

By Bhomvrat Rai • October 30, 2026 • topoteretes/cognee#6382

Architectural Overview

Modern international businesses manage multi-currency bank accounts, supplier payouts, and cross-border treasury movements on Airwallex. An agent operating in accounts payable or executive operations requires grounding in payout statuses, settlement records, and currency balances. This connector synchronizes Airwallex data into Cognee Knowledge Graphs.

Key Capabilities

  • Cross-Border Payout Records: Ingests beneficiary names, bank networks, transfer amounts, payment reasons, and settlement timestamps.
  • Multi-Currency Balance Tracking: Pulls ledger availability across USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, SGD, and HKD.
  • Deterministic Entity Hashing: Computes stable SHA-256 node IDs to ensure seamless incremental updates.

Usage Example

from cognee_community_connector_airwallex import AirwallexConnector

connector = AirwallexConnector(api_token="YOUR_BEARER_TOKEN")
documents = connector.fetch_documents(page_size=50)

print(f"Ingested {len(documents)} financial documents into Cognee.")
for doc in documents[:2]:
    print(doc["metadata"])
    print(doc["text"])
