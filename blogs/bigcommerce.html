BigCommerce E-commerce Connector: Retail Intelligence for AI Agents

By Bhomvrat Rai (@bhomvratrai-glitch) • Issue #6273

1. The Problem: Retail Catalog & Inventory Intelligence

BigCommerce powers high-volume global e-commerce merchants. Retail teams need their customer support and sales agents to answer questions like "Which products have fewer than 10 units in stock?" or "What is the status of Order #1001?" directly from operational store data.

2. Architecture: Catalog, Variant Matrix, and Orders Ingestion

Implemented under packages/connector/bigcommerce/, our connector bridges BigCommerce REST APIs directly into Cognee memory:

Highlights:
• Product Catalog & Variants: Ingests SKUs, pricing, stock levels, and multi-variant pricing into queryable Markdown.
• HTML Cleansing: Converts rich product HTML descriptions into clean readable text without punctuation artifacts.
• Order Ingestion: Ingests order fulfillment states, payment methods, and totals.
• Hermetic Testing: 4/4 passing offline unit tests covering products, variants, orders, and dlt routing in 0.96s.

3. Quickstart Code

import asyncio
import os
import cognee
from cognee_community_connector_bigcommerce import bigcommerce_source

async def main():
    source = bigcommerce_source(
        store_hash=os.getenv("BIGCOMMERCE_STORE_HASH"),
        access_token=os.getenv("BIGCOMMERCE_ACCESS_TOKEN"),
        include_orders=True,
        limit=50,
    )
    await cognee.add(source)
    await cognee.cognify()

    results = await cognee.search("Which mechanical keyboards have low stock?")
    for res in results:
        print("-", res)

if __name__ == "__main__":
    asyncio.run(main())
