Integrating CoinGecko Cryptocurrency Market Intelligence into Cognee

By Bhomvrat Rai • Issue #6331 • 5 min read

Cryptocurrency prices, 24-hour trading volumes, market capitalizations, and circulating supplies fluctuate continuously across global decentralized and centralized exchanges. For autonomous financial AI agents and AI research copilots, having real-time and historical crypto market data embedded into Cognee's semantic memory and knowledge graphs enables answering questions like "What was the market cap trend of Solana during Q3?" or "Compare the 24h trading volume of Bitcoin vs Ethereum."

Key Architectural Highlights

  • Public Tier Zero-Auth: Allows developers to instantly connect and pull live cryptocurrency market data without requiring API keys or paid subscriptions.
  • Deterministic Document Keying: Generates stable SHA-256 identifiers keyed by coin identifier and snapshot timestamp ({coin_id}_{last_updated}) to prevent duplicated graph nodes.
  • Incremental Sync Cursors: Filters incoming snapshot records against an ISO 8601 cursor to ingest only fresh price movements, saving significant graph indexing overhead.
  • Structured Semantic Markdown: Translates high/low metrics, volume, market cap ranks, and circulating supplies into human- and LLM-readable markdown blocks.

Usage Example

from cognee_community_connector_coingecko import CoinGeckoConnector

connector = CoinGeckoConnector(vs_currency="usd")

# Ingest top market assets
documents = connector.fetch_documents(
    ids=["bitcoin", "ethereum", "solana"],
    per_page=10
)

for doc in documents:
    print(f"Ingested Coin: {doc['metadata']['name']} (${doc['metadata']['symbol']})")
    print(doc["text"])
