RevenueCat In-App Subscriptions & Entitlements Connector for Cognee

By Bhomvrat Rai • October 30, 2026 • topoteretes/cognee#6358

Architectural Overview

Mobile engineering teams building cross-platform apps rely on RevenueCat for subscriber state management across Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and Stripe Web Billing. This connector brings subscriber lifecycle metrics, plan purchases, expiration milestones, and churn signals directly into Cognee Knowledge Graphs.

Key Capabilities

  • Multi-Store Entitlement Extraction: Unifies active entitlements across iOS and Android ecosystems.
  • Subscription Churn Signals: Tracks unsubscribe detection dates, cancellations, and active renewal dates.
  • Semantic Customer Profiles: Converts raw JSON subscriber payloads into queryable Markdown graphs.

Usage Example

from cognee_community_connector_revenuecat import RevenueCatConnector

connector = RevenueCatConnector(api_key="YOUR_REVENUECAT_SECRET_KEY")
documents = connector.fetch_documents_for_users(["user_premium_123"])

print(f"Ingested {len(documents)} subscription documents into Cognee.")
for doc in documents:
    print(doc["metadata"])
    print(doc["text"])
